Plasma Fusion · Settings, part 2

Touchpad gestures

The touchpad gestures KWin leaves free, each set to what it does: a group on the Plasma Fusion settings page, after Tablet. Three fingers up and down keep what they do today, four fingers pinched in open the launcher and spread out show the desktop, as on a Mac; three fingers left and right do nothing until set; the desktops stay in one row, as the setup puts them (KWin's rows), which leaves up and down to these rows with several desktops. Every row stays yours to set: while KWin also uses a gesture (three fingers left and right switch desktops while there is more than one column of them, up and down while there is more than one row), its row says so, and both happen.

What KWin takes. KWin 6.7.5 handles every touchpad gesture with three or more fingers and gives apps the two-finger ones. Its own: four fingers up (Overview), down (its grid), left and right (desktops); a three-finger pinch (zoom); three fingers left and right while there is more than one column of desktops, up and down while there is more than one row. None of them can be turned off (6.7.5): the group offers the others, and the rows KWin uses now and then stay set, with a note while it does. Turned off, the desktops get back the rows they had (KWin's default is two).
Sizes. Rows 44 px with the page's 13 px labels and group title. The drop-down is the Controls board's (here 34 px, the page's control height; radius 10, surface2 with a 1 px border, a 14 px chevron, the 2 px focus ring while its list is open); the list is the Controls board's menu (radius 14, 6 px padding, 32 px items, the chosen one in the accent's soft fill with a check). "Keep desktops in one row" is the page's switch row (40 x 22, 18 px knob). No new colour.
What they do. Each choice is something the desktop already does by a shortcut: Overview, Show desktop, the grid of desktops and All windows are KWin's; Launcher is the Meta key's; quick settings and notifications open as with their own keys; Snap layouts is Meta+Z's picker. Nothing leaves the gesture to no one.
