Plasma Fusion · Dock, part 2

Dock size and window grouping

Three dock sizes on the settings page, and a switch that gives each window its own icon. Medium is today's dock, unchanged; Large is the Tablet board's 56 px dock; Small is the one new size. The magnified size follows the dock: the page's choice is a step (+8 or +14 px) added to the icons, so magnification still shows at Large. In tablet posture the dock keeps its own 56 px tiles.

Magnified icon size
Sizes. Small: 40 px icons 7 px apart, 30 px separators (3 px margins), 10 px from the plate's sides, 12 below; the fixed buttons 40 px with an 11 px radius and 20 px glyphs. Medium: the Main board, unchanged. Large: the Tablet board's metrics (56 px, 12 px apart, 40 px separators, 6 x 4 dot and 18 x 4 pill 4 px under the icon). The plate (the panel) is 60, 72 or 78 px; its corners are the Plasma style's one dock frame, 24 px at every size.
Magnified size. The page's +8 / +14 px is the step at Medium and Large (56 and 62 px at Medium, as today; 64 and 70 at Large). Below 48 px the step shrinks with the icon (Small: +7 or +12, 47 and 52 px), so a small dock does not swell past its neighbours. The neighbours follow the dock's falloff (3/7 of the step at the next icon).
Window count. Grouped, the marks under an icon count its windows, three at most: running dots 3 px apart, and for the active app the pill first. Grouping off, each window is its own icon with one mark; no title on hover (the name pill keeps the app's name). The app's menu, previews and audio button act on that window; a pinned app's windows stay at its pin. The marks' sizes and colours are the Main board's.
