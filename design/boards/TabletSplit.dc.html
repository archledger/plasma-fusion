One app, and an app for the other side

In tablet posture, one app in a half leaves the rest of the screen to the home screen. There, instead of a home page cut off under the app, your apps wait for the other side: tap one and it opens beside the first, as on Android and iPadOS.

When it showsIn tablet posture, when one app fills the left or the right part of the screen, top to bottom, and nothing else is shown beside it: a split from the dock or an app's menu with nothing else open, or the other app of a split closed or sent home. It goes as soon as a second app is beside the first, the first leaves its side, or the posture ends.
A tapThe app opens on this side, beside the first; one that is already running comes here. The app on the other side is dimmed. Home apps first, in the order of the home pages, then every app A to Z; the list scrolls.
Open windows firstWith other windows open, “Pick a window for this side” covers this half right after the split (board 16, Title-bar tabs and snapping); once it is dismissed, this picker is under it. Esc or a tap on the wallpaper does nothing here: the home gesture goes home, as anywhere.
