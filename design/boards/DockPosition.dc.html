Plasma Fusion · Dock, part 3

The dock on the left or right

Dock position on the settings page: Bottom (as on the Main board), Left or Right. On a side the dock is the same plate turned upright: Start, Search and Overview at the top, the apps, Downloads and Trash at the bottom, 16 px from the screen's edge and centred on the screen's height. It magnifies along the edge and grows inwards; its name pill, previews, menus and the launcher open beside it. In tablet mode the dock is at the bottom, as on the Tablet board, and goes back to its side after.

Dock size
Geometry. The bottom dock turned upright, at every size: the plate 72 px wide (Medium), 14 px of padding on the edge side, 10 inside, 12 at the ends; tiles 8 px apart; the separators 36 x 1 px, 4 px margins, centred on the tiles. The plate stands 16 px from the screen's edge (the bottom dock's 16 px from the bottom), and the 16 px inside it are its headroom. It is centred on the screen's height (the compositor places a panel that reserves no room on the whole screen) and keeps clear of the top bar: with more apps than fit, the icons shrink as on the bottom dock.
Marks and magnification. The running marks are on the edge side, 5 px from the icon, upright: 4 x 5 dots, the 4 x 16 pill, the window count stacked 3 px apart. The icon under the pointer grows inwards from the edge side (62 px at Medium, its neighbours 54), and the dock grows along the edge. The name pill, 12 px beside the magnified icon, is the Main board's; previews and menus open beside the dock too.
Launcher and tablet mode. Start opens the launcher beside the dock, 8 px from the plate and centred on the dock's height, below the top bar; its look is the Launcher board's. With the dock at the bottom it stays centred, as now. In tablet mode the dock is at the bottom with the Tablet board's strip, and goes back to its side when tablet mode ends. A dock on every screen takes the main dock's position.
