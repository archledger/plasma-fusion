Plasma Fusion · Top bar, part 2

Live chips

What uses the microphone, the camera or the screen, who controls this computer or has signed in to it over SSH, and what runs on its own (a screen recording, a Keep awake timer) show as chips at the start of the top bar's right cluster, in the bar's own sizes and the Colors board's semantic colours. Nothing in use: the bar is as today. A click or a hover opens the panel under the chips; it names the apps and stops what can be stopped. The tray items these signals come from stay hidden, as the other replaced items do.

Sizes. Chips 22 px high, radius 11, 6 px from the next item, glyphs 14 px (the bar's 16 px icons one step down), numbers in Space Grotesk 600 12 px as the clock. The panel is the Popups board's (360 px, radius 22, 16 px padding, rows 48 px, radius 12).
Colours. Microphone ForegroundNeutral (macOS's orange), camera ForegroundPositive (green), screen sharing ForegroundVisited (violet), recording and remote control ForegroundNegative (red), SSH ForegroundLink (blue); a single kind tints the capsule with its badge tint. No new colour.
Where it comes from. The microphone: plasma-pa's recording streams, an app's own Mute. The camera: PipeWire's camera monitor, the app from its stream. The portal's "Screen casting" and "Remote Control" items (their End action) and Spectacle's recording item (its time; a click stops it). SSH: systemd-logind's sessions with Remote=yes (sshd, tailscaled), active ones only, with user and host. Keep awake: quick settings' timer; a click opens it.
