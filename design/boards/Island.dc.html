Plasma Fusion · Top bar, part 3

The top bar hidden, and the island

Hide the top bar automatically, on the settings page (off by default): the bar slides away and a maximized window takes the whole screen; the pointer at the top of the screen brings it back over the window. While it is hidden, an island after Apple's Dynamic Island sits at the top centre: what uses the camera, the microphone or the screen always, then a screen recording, a Keep awake timer or the music playing, at most two at once; a click opens it in place. Nothing in use: no island. With the bar shown, in tablet mode, or with the switch off, there is no island.

After Apple's Dynamic Island. As Apple's Human Interface Guidelines (Live Activities) set it out: one thing shows compact, a leading part that names it and a trailing part with its state, read as one; with two, the second sits detached in a bubble beside it; a click opens the island in place, an enlarged version that keeps the parts where they were. Opaque black in light and dark, bold colours on it, and over dark windows a 1 px key line tinted to its content. The privacy dots sit inside it, as on iPhone. A click on an opened island's free part, outside it or Esc closes it again.
Sizes. Compact: 32 px high, radius 16, at least 132 px wide, 6 px margins (the 20 px leading parts sit concentric in them), the trailing text in Space Grotesk 600 13 px; 4 px from the top of the screen and centred on it, where the clock pill is. The bubble: 32 px, 6 px beside it. Opened: 400 px wide, 84 to 160 px high for its content, radius 28, 14 px margins (rounded parts inside radius 14), 30 px buttons. Dots 8 px, 4 px apart; in a full-screen window 6 px in a 14 px capsule. It morphs between them with the Motion tokens' durations, well under Apple's 2 s.
What it shows. What uses the camera, the microphone or the screen always comes first, in the LiveChips board's colours (camera green, microphone orange, screen violet, remote control red, SSH blue); then one of a screen recording (opened: Stop), a Keep awake timer and the music playing (opened: play, pause, skip). The rest waits in the bar and quick settings. In a full-screen window only the privacy dots show. Plasma's own pop-ups (volume, brightness and the rest) show in it too, below. Not in the island: notifications, the clock, file copies (the dock shows them).
Plasma's notices. While the bar is hidden, what Plasma's own pop-up would show (the box two thirds down the screen) shows in the island instead, and the box does not: the volume (to Plasma's maximum, the tick at 100 %, warning to 125, danger past it; muted: Audio Muted), the microphone, a player's volume, the screen and keyboard brightness, the keyboard layout, the touchpad, the on-screen keyboard, the power mode, sleep blocked, Night Light, Do Not Disturb. Plasma's icon decides the glyph, its text the words; for 1.8 s, as Plasma's own, replacing what the island shows for that time (then it comes back), also over a full-screen window. With the bar shown, Plasma's own box, as now; turned off in Plasma, no notices either. Level: the glyph leading, a 96 px bar and the number trailing.
The bar and the island. The bar comes back when the pointer touches the top of the screen and stays while the pointer is on it or one of its menus or panels is open, as Plasma's auto-hide panels do; the island fades out as it slides in and comes back when it hides. It cannot be dismissed while something is in use, and it stays above full-screen windows. Every screen with a hidden top bar has its own. Off (the default) and in tablet mode the bar always shows, as now.
