Hold and drag the pillIn tablet posture, hold the app's pill in the top bar for 0.3 s and drag down: the two halves show over the app (the dock's split drag) and the app's icon follows the finger. Released over the left or right third, the app takes that half and the other half offers what fills it; over the middle third it is full screen; in the bottom 96 px it goes home. Dragged back into the top bar, it stays as it was.
A flickWithout waiting for the halves: the pill flicked sideways (800 px/s or faster, as the home screen's swipe) puts the app in that half at once, as a flick does on iPadOS. A short tap still opens the window card, and the 24 px pull-down too.
Full screen offWith apps full screen (the default), switching Full screen off in the window card puts the app in a half instead of leaving it floating out of reach: the free half, the right one when both are free; the other half then offers apps. In the windowed mode windows still float, and the same drag moves them. A half narrower than the app's minimum width is not offered.
